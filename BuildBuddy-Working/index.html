    THE QUICK GUIDE

    PC building, in plain English.

    You do not need to know everything before you start. Here are the basics that matter.

    01

    Parts must fit each other

    The CPU and motherboard need the same socket. RAM must match the board’s memory type, and the motherboard must fit inside the case.

    02

    Power and space matter

    The power supply needs enough capacity with some headroom. Long graphics cards and tall coolers must fit inside the case.

    03

    Spend where it counts

    For gaming, the graphics card usually has the biggest impact. For editing, the CPU and memory can matter more.

    04

    Airflow keeps it cool

    Cool air comes in at the front and warm air leaves at the back or top. A few good fans are more useful than lots of cheap ones.
